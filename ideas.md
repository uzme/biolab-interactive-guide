# BioLab Interactive Guide — Design Philosophy & Architecture

## Approach Chosen: "Modern Precision Biotech" (Crisp, high-end laboratory interface with deep scientific credibility)

- **Theme Name**: Modern Precision Biotech
- **Very Brief Intro**: A pristine, high-end scientific interface blending deep teal/emerald scientific accents with immaculate white/slate typography, designed for professional biotechnologists and researchers.
- **Probability**: 0.05
- **Design Movement**: Contemporary Swiss Scientific UI & Glassmorphic Precision
- **Core Principles**:
  1. **Uncompromising Clarity**: Every piece of equipment data, technical parameter, and price benchmark is structured with absolute visual hierarchy.
  2. **Tactile Interactivity**: Seamless 3D viewer integration, interactive hotspots, and step-by-step guided tours.
  3. **Biotexnolog Aesthetic**: Deep teal, emerald, and clean slate gradients reflecting modern laboratory standards.
- **Color Philosophy**: 
  - Primary: Deep Emerald/Teal (`#0d9488` / `oklch(0.55 0.15 180)`) for precision and biological trust.
  - Secondary: Slate Dark (`#1e293b`) for structural authority.
  - Accent: Vibrant Mint (`#14b8a6`) for interactive highlights and hotspot pulses.
- **Layout Paradigm**: Asymmetric sidebar navigation combined with a rich card grid and immersive detail drawer/modal views for 3D model exploration.
- **Signature Elements**: Glowing hotspot pulses on 3D models, categorical badge pills, structured financial/import budget breakdowns, and step-by-step guided tour overlays.
- **Typography System**: Plus Jakarta Sans is dominant for all headings, labels, and equipment titles; serif display typography is excluded from the core identity.

## Style Decisions

- Prioritize a cold, pristine lab-grade canvas: white, cool slate, translucent glass panels, teal signal accents, and thin technical separators.
- Every equipment card must show at least one scientific specificity cue, such as a model-preview treatment, parameter chip, hotspot marker, or compact technical data band.
- The hero must communicate interactive exploration immediately through model-viewer, hotspot, guided-tour, or calibration language rather than functioning only as a promotional banner.
